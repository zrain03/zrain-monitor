Zrain Monitor is developed by Zrain. It processes your Firebase authentication identity and the public HTTPS URLs you register for monitoring.

Information stored

For monitoring, we store your Firebase account identifier, monitor name and URL, status, response time, timestamps, check history, incidents, and in-app notifications and, when you enable email alerts, your verified email address and alert delivery status. Opt-in email alerts use a third-party email delivery provider. We do not intentionally store response bodies, passwords, or confidential endpoint headers.

Why we use this information

To run scheduled checks, provide your private dashboard, display incidents, enforce usage limits and protect the service from abuse. The service sends periodic HTTP requests to your registered URLs. These requests may be visible in your hosting provider's access logs.

Storage and processors

Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Website hosting uses Firebase Hosting; the monitoring API and database use Cloudflare Workers and D1. If you enable optional email alerts, Resend processes the email address and alert message to deliver them. Optional Telegram alerts use Telegram Bot API; after you explicitly connect your account, we store your Telegram chat identifier and delivery status in D1 and send alert text to Telegram. Disconnect in Settings or send /stop to the bot to revoke the link. Their infrastructure and privacy policies also apply.

Retention

Detailed checks are retained for approximately 30 days, with daily summaries retained for up to 90 days. Incidents and monitor data remain until deletion, subject to maintenance and abuse investigation requirements.

Deletion

Go to Settings in Zrain Monitor and select Delete monitor data & account. If Firebase requires recent authentication, please sign in again before deletion. To request help, email zrain.dev@gmail.com.

Limitations

Checks are best-effort and may be delayed, interrupted or unavailable when free-tier quotas or platform limits are reached. Keep-Awake requests cannot guarantee that another provider will keep a service running.

Telegram notifications are off until you connect your Telegram chat and enable alerts for a specific monitor. Email notifications require a configured email provider; the public beta currently prioritizes Telegram.
